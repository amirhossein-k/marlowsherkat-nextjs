# Product gallery and zoom

The product cards now support:

- Click-to-open product gallery
- Three gallery entries per product using the sample product visuals
- Full-screen responsive lightbox
- Previous/next navigation
- Thumbnail navigation
- Close button
- Accessible dialog labels and button labels
- Mobile-friendly gallery sizing

The gallery is implemented in `app/page.tsx` and styled in `app/globals.css`. Replace the sample image URLs in the `products` array with official Marlow photography when available.
