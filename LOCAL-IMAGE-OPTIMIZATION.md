# Local product images

Product visuals are now stored locally in `public/products` and loaded with local paths:

- `executive-chair.svg`
- `gaming-chair.svg`
- `ergonomic-chair.svg`

SVG was chosen because these are generated sample visuals: it keeps the package tiny, sharp on retina screens, and fast to load. The files are reused for the hero, product cards, gallery, and portfolio blocks. Replace them with official WebP/AVIF product photos later if available.
